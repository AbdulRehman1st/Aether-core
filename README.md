
A free, balanced arcade clicker that runs in your browser. Tap the core, build generators, trigger Fever Mode and ascend for star shards.

**Play now:** https://abdulrehman1st.github.io/Aether-core/

## How to play

- **Tap the core** (or press Space or Enter) to harvest gems.
- **Buy generators** to earn gems every second. Use the x1, x10, x100 and MAX buttons to buy in bulk.
- **Buy super upgrades** to boost tap power, production and crit chance.
- **Use Fever Mode** when the heat bar is full. It gives x2 production for 15 seconds.
- **Catch golden orbs** when they appear. They give a bonus worth about 20 seconds of income.
- **Ascend** with Cosmic Supernova once you have earned 5M gems in a run. You keep star shards, and each shard gives +2% production forever.

## Balance rules

- Taps are capped at 10 per second.
- Generators are the main income. Taps give a small share.
- Combo caps at x1.5 and resets after 2 seconds without tapping.
- Crit chance starts at 5% (x3 damage) and tops out at 15%.
- Every generator doubles its output at levels 25, 50, 100, 150 and 200.
- Offline progress gives 50% income for up to 2 hours.

## Saving

Progress saves automatically in your browser (localStorage). Clearing your browser data erases it. You can reset your save from the settings button.

## Run it yourself

It is a single file. Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser.
